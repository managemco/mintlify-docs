## Seller quickstart

If you want to sell on Managem, start with onboarding. It takes a few minutes and unlocks payouts.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Create your account">
    Sign up on [Managem](https://managem.co.uk) and log in.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Complete onboarding">
    Follow **[Getting started](/sellers/getting-started)** to finish Stripe Connect onboarding.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Start listing">
    Use the **[Seller overview](/sellers/index)** to publish listings and begin selling.
  </Step>
</Steps>
