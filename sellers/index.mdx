[Managem](https://managem.co.uk), is a UK (only) trading card marketplace built for both **individuals and businesses**.

Whether you're clearing out your binder, running a local shop, or managing a professional storefront — selling on Managem is fast, simple, and rewarding.

---

## Why Sell on Managem?

- **0% Commission Fees** — keep 100% of your sale price.
- **For Everyone** — individuals and businesses welcome.
- **Secure Payments** — powered by [Stripe Connect](https://stripe.com/connect).
- **Quick KYC** — Stripe's hosted onboarding ensures compliance and trust.
- **Start in Minutes** — the whole setup takes just a few clicks.

---

## Getting Started

<Steps>
  <Step title="Sign up and create your account">
    Create your Managem account to get started. You can sign up with your email
    or use a Discord account.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click 'Start Selling'">
    **Complete onboarding** through Stripe's hosted KYC flow. This includes
    providing some basic information about you and your business (if
    applicable).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Create your listings">
    Either create single listings for cards and sealed product, bulk create
    listings or _import your listings from elsewhere_ **(coming soon)**
  </Step>
  <Step title="Sell with confidence">
    Buyers pay securely through Stripe, and payouts are handled automatically.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Get paid">
    Funds are transferred to your bank when you withdraw.
  </Step>
</Steps>
