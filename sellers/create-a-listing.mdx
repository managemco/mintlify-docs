Once your seller onboarding is complete, you're ready to put items up for sale. This guide walks through creating a **single listing** — perfect for one-off cards or small batches. If you've got hundreds or thousands of items, jump to [Imports](/sellers/imports) instead.

<Info>
  Before you create your first listing, make sure you've finished
  [onboarding through Stripe Connect](/sellers/getting-started). You won't be
  able to publish listings until your seller account is active.
</Info>

## The quick version

<Steps>
  <Step title="Find the item in the catalogue">
    Search by card name, set, or paste in a [URN](/glossary). Managem's
    catalogue covers Pokémon, Magic: The Gathering, Lorcana, Star Wars
    Unlimited and Riftbound.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pick the condition">
    Select the grade that best matches the item. See
    [Grading your cards](/sellers/grading-your-cards) for the full definitions
    of each grade.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set your price and quantity">
    Enter your price in pounds and the number of copies you have available.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add details and photos">
    Fill in any relevant comments, private notes, and — for listings priced
    £20+ or sealed items with a seal tear — upload clear photos.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Publish">
    Review everything, and click **Publish**. Your listing is live immediately
    and visible in search results.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Finding the right item

Managem stores every printing of every card (and every sealed product) as a distinct **item** in the catalogue. Your listing attaches to an item, so it's important to pick the correct one.

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Search by name">
    Start typing the card or product name in the listing form. The suggestions
    dropdown shows matching items with their set and collector number so you
    can confirm you're picking the right printing (for example, first-edition
    vs. unlimited, or promo vs. base set).
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Search by set and number">
    If you know the exact set and collector number, filter by **game** and
    **set** first, then scroll to the number. This is usually the fastest way
    to find reprints.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Paste a URN or external ID">
    If you already have a [URN](/glossary) (for example from an integration or
    spreadsheet), paste it directly into the search box. You can also use a
    **Cardmarket ID** or **TCGplayer ID** if you've mapped your inventory
    from another marketplace.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

<Warning>
  If you can't find your item in the catalogue, please reach out on
  [Discord](https://discord.gg/qaU4KSEQRd) or at
  [support@managem.co.uk](mailto:support@managem.co.uk) — don't list it under
  a similar-looking item, as this can lead to
  [SNAD](/glossary) disputes.
</Warning>

## Choosing the condition

Pick from the seven supported grades:

- **Mint (MT)** — factory-fresh to the naked eye.
- **Near Mint (NM)** — looks new at arm's length.
- **Excellent (EX)** — light to moderate play, still sleeve-playable.
- **Good (GD)** — moderate play; a very light crease may be acceptable.
- **Lightly Played (LP)** — significant play but still sleeve-playable.
- **Played (PL)** — heavy wear, identifiable, sleeve-usable but poor.
- **Poor (PO)** — not sleeve-playable; heavy structural damage.

Full definitions, photos of each grade and special cases (foils, graded slabs, sealed products) live on the [Grading your cards](/sellers/grading-your-cards) page.

<Tip>
  Managem doesn't support partial grades (like NM- or EX+). If your card sits
  between two grades, pick the lower grade and describe the nuance in the
  **comments** field.
</Tip>

## Pricing and quantity

- **Price** is in pounds (e.g. `3.50`). Listings are paid out in GBP.
- **Quantity** is the number of _identical copies_ you have in the same condition. If you have two NM copies and one LP copy, create two separate listings.
- **0% commission** — Managem takes no percentage of the sale. Stripe may charge a small payout fee depending on your region.

<Tip>
  Not sure what to charge? Managem shows recent sale prices and current active
  listings on the item's detail page — use these as a benchmark.
</Tip>

## Photos and comments

Photos aren't required for every listing, but are **mandatory** in two cases:

- Any listing priced **£20 or more**.
- **Sealed products with any seal tear** (regardless of price).

Clear photos measurably reduce dispute rates, so we recommend adding them even for cheaper listings.

Use the **comments** field to record anything a buyer should know before purchase: signatures, light creases, off-centre printing, language/edition nuances, or included extras.

Use the **notes** field for anything you want to remember but buyers shouldn't see (your SKU, shelf location, etc.).

## Variants

Some cards and products ship in multiple flavours — foil vs. non-foil, different languages, reverse holos, alternate art, different packaging for sealed boxes. When you pick an item, you'll see a **Variants** selector if one applies. Pick the exact variant you're listing; selling the wrong variant is a common cause of [SNAD](/glossary) cases.

## Publishing

When you click **Publish**, the listing goes live immediately. Buyers can add it to their basket and checkout right away, so make sure:

- The condition matches the item in front of you.
- You actually have the quantity you've entered in stock.
- You can [dispatch within 3 business days](/sellers/packing-your-order) (the default) or have adjusted your dispatch time.

## After publishing

- **Edit** — you can change price, quantity and comments at any time from your **Listings** dashboard.
- **Hide** — toggle the visibility flag to remove a listing from search results without deleting it (useful if you're low on stock or on vacation).
- **Sell** — when a buyer checks out, you'll get a notification and the order appears in your **Orders** dashboard. Head to [Packing your order](/sellers/packing-your-order) for what to do next.

<Tip>
  Selling a lot at once? Skip the single-listing form and jump straight to
  **[Imports](/sellers/imports)**. You can upload a CSV to create or update
  thousands of listings in one go.
</Tip>
